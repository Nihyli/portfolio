## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are recruiters, hiring managers, engineers, and technical leads evaluating Yohannes Hailu for internships and software engineering roles. They typically scan quickly on desktop first, then revisit sections on mobile when sharing links.

### Brand Personality
Technical, confident, clean. The site should feel capable and modern without looking overdesigned, gimmicky, or AI-generated.

### Aesthetic Direction
Single-page portfolio with a light editorial base and subtle technical accents inspired by the provided references. Keep structure clear and readable, with restrained motion, strong hierarchy, and a dedicated hero portrait placeholder for a future image.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with signal: concise value proposition, measurable outcomes, and high-impact projects first.
2. Keep rhythm intentional: generous whitespace, clear section boundaries, and readable text measure.
3. Use subtle technical cues: fine grid/noise accents, mono labels, and precise micro-interactions.
4. Preserve credibility: avoid trendy gimmicks, loud gradients, and generic card spam.
5. Design for scanning: headings, metadata, and call-to-actions should be obvious in under 10 seconds.
